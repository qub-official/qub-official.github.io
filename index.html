01 About

A little about me 소개

I'm qub. I've been hunting on HackerOne since 2022, on public and private programs, and I care most about one rule: revoked access should stay revoked.

I test web apps and APIs for authorization flaws: sessions and tokens that outlive their owners, roles that leak data, and checks that run after the data is already gone. Every report I send has a clear proof of concept and steps anyone can reproduce.

I also build software. KellyBlue is my recon automation platform for authorized testing, and my Windows apps (an editor, a terminal, a downloader, a media player, a password manager, a learning Bible, a beat maker, a Christian song checker and more) are free and designed to feel soft, quiet and fast.

You may know me by my old name, kellyblue. Everywhere I operate now, I'm qub.
